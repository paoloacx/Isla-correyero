Biography

Isla Correyero (Miajadas, Cáceres, 1957) studied journalism and film in Madrid and works professionally as a film and television screenwriter. Her credits include the series Quinta planta, directed by Antonio Mercero. She also worked for thirteen years as a nurse at Madrid’s Ramón y Cajal University Hospital, an experience that runs through much of her writing.

Poetry and the stage

She made her debut in 1984 with Cráter (Colección Provincia, León), which won the Villafranca del Bierzo Prize. That same year she published the chapbook Una taza de caldo (Pliego de El Crotalón). Early works were followed by Lianas (Hiperión, 1988), Crímenes (Libertarias, 1993) and the booklet Isla Correyero (Centro Cultural de la Generación del 27, Málaga, 1993), with a foreword by Juan Carlos Mestre.

In 1996 she published Diario de una enfermera (Huerga & Fierro), a work born of direct contact with suffering in hospital and awarded the Córdoba City «Ricardo Molina» Poetry Prize. Two years later came La Pasión (Ex Libris, 1998; finalist for the Fernando Rielo World Prize for Mystical Poetry) and the chapbook Como cuando coges una trucha en las manos (Ediciones Piratas, Galaroza, 1998).

In the 21st century she published Amor tirano (DVD Ediciones, 2002; Hermanos Argensola Prize) and Género humano (2014), released on Amazon and now published as Lepidópteros. In 2015 came Hoz en la espalda (Evolución de un divorcio) (Huerga & Fierro), later reissued as Divorcio and staged as a dramatic opera at Salamanca’s Teatro Juan del Enzina in 2014.

Her recent work includes the selected poems Mi bien (Visor, 2018), edited with a foreword by Juan Antonio González Iglesias, and the poetry collection Japonesas (Visor, 2021), which won the 31st Jaime Gil de Biedma Poetry Prize.

Anthologies and current work

Her work has appeared in some of the major anthologies of contemporary Spanish poetry: Las diosas blancas (Hiperión, 1986), Ellas tienen la palabra (Hiperión, 1997), Humanismo solidario. Poesía y compromiso en la sociedad contemporánea (Visor, 2014) and Poesía soy yo (Visor, 2016). As an editor, she also brought together Feroces: muestra de las actitudes radicales, marginales y heterodoxas en la última poesía española (DVD Ediciones, 1998).

She has taken part in readings and international events at Madrid’s Royal Palace (2000), the European Academy of Yuste Foundation (2004), Rotterdam’s Poetry International Festival with Seamus Heaney (2005) and the Ibero-American Summit in Salamanca (2008).

She is currently preparing her collected works and a new book of poetry.
